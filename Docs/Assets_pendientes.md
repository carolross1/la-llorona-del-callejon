# Inventario de Assets Pendientes y Guía de Generación

**Proyecto:** La Llorona del Callejón  
**Equipo:** Carol y Princes  
**Motor:** Unity 6 Universal 2D (URP)  

Este documento lista con precisión los recursos audiovisuales y gráficos necesarios para completar los Niveles 2, 3 y 4, con recomendaciones de búsqueda libre y prompts detallados para generación con IA.

## 2. Sprites Pendientes para Niveles 2, 3 y 4 (Prompts para IA)

* **Estilo visual unificado:** Pixel art 2D top-down (vista cenital ligeramente angular, perspectiva RPG), paleta oscura y saturada dominada por tonos morados nocturnos, azules profundos, negros y toques cálidos en puntos de luz. Fondo liso de un solo color (#000000 o #00FF00) para recorte limpio.

### 2.1. Nivel 2: El Callejón del Beso
* **Faroles Coloniales de Pared:**
  * *Prompt IA:* `Pixel art top-down 2D prop, colonial Mexican wrought iron wall lantern, dark purple and deep bronze metal, warm dim orange candle inside, top-down perspective, 192x192 px, dark horror atmosphere, solid black background, crisp pixel edges --no anti-aliasing`
* **Puertas y Zaguanes Coloniales de Madera:**
  * *Prompt IA:* `Pixel art top-down 2D colonial wooden double door, rustic aged dark wood, wrought iron studs, dark stone frame, gothic Mexican colonial architecture, dark purple ambient tint, top-down perspective, solid pure green background #00FF00, crisp pixel edges`
* **Barriles y Lonas de Escondite:**
  * *Prompt IA:* `Pixel art top-down prop, cluster of vintage wooden barrels draped with a dark canvas tarp, stealth hiding spot, dark nocturnal color scheme, top-down RPG view, solid black background, clean pixel art`

### 2.2. Nivel 3: El Atrio de la Parroquia
* **Fuente Central de Cantera:**
  * *Prompt IA:* `Pixel art top-down 2D circular colonial stone fountain, carved quarry stone basin, dark still water with reflections, gothic Mexican plaza decor, dark navy and purple moonlight palette, 384x384 px, top-down view, solid black background`
* **Fachada de Parroquia / Iglesia:**
  * *Prompt IA:* `Pixel art 2D facade segment of historic baroque Mexican church, Dolores Hidalgo style, carved quarry stone arches, ornate dark wooden church entrance, nocturnal dark purple sky lighting, top-down angular perspective, solid green background`
* **Árboles de Plaza Colonial:**
  * *Prompt IA:* `Pixel art top-down large colonial plaza tree, dense canopy of leaves casting dark shadows, twisted trunk, spooky night lighting, purple and teal highlights, 256x256 px, solid black background`
* **Bancas de Hierro Forjado:**
  * *Prompt IA:* `Pixel art top-down wrought iron park bench, ornate curved arms, colonial Dolores Hidalgo style, dark metal with faint cyan highlights, solid black background`

### 2.3. Nivel 4: El Cementerio
* **Lápidas y Cruces de Cantera:**
  * *Prompt IA:* `Pixel art top-down graveyard tombstones, cracked quarry stone crosses and headstones, overgrown with moss, eerie night atmosphere, moody purple and slate blue lighting, top-down RPG perspective, solid black background`
* **Criptas y Mausoleos Familiares:**
  * *Prompt IA:* `Pixel art top-down stone mausoleum crypt, ornate gothic colonial Mexican tomb, heavy stone door, candle holders, dark eerie lighting, 256x256 px, solid green background`
* **Puerta de la Casa de Carlos (Meta Final):**
  * *Prompt IA:* `Pixel art 2D traditional Mexican village house facade with a welcoming rustic wooden door, warm light spilling from a small window, safe haven contrast against dark night, top-down view, solid black background`
* **Niebla Animada / Textura de Partículas:**
  * *Prompt IA:* `Seamless tileable low-opacity pixel art spooky ground fog texture, wispy smoke tendrils, purple and pale blue ethereal haze, seamless pattern, black background`
